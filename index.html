FAQ

Questions worth answering

Is Clipt free?

Yes. The Free plan gives you 15 active links, 5 new links per day, and 1 custom alias. Premium is a small flat fee paid via bKash, Nagad, or Rocket, and unlocks 500 links, 100 per day, and 50 aliases. See Pricing.

Do you track my visitors?

No cookies, no fingerprints, no cross-site identifiers. On a click, we record only the day, and — for premium link owners — the visitor's country and referring hostname. Bots are filtered out of your counts entirely.

Do free links expire?

Yes. Free links automatically expire after 90 days. Premium links can be permanent, or you can set your own expiry date. Expired links show a clear "this link has expired" page rather than a broken error.

What happens if I forget my password?

An admin can reset it for you. Clipt doesn't have self-service password reset yet — this avoids the security risks of email link flows. Reach out through the contact channel on the Pricing page.

How do I upgrade to Premium?

Send the fee via bKash, Nagad, or Rocket, then submit the transaction ID from your dashboard. An admin reviews and approves — usually within a few hours.
